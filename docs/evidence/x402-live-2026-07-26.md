| Role | Account | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Agent (payer) | `0.0.9708355` | Buys the beneficiary check |

The `@x402/hedera` scheme enforces this separation itself: it rejected a
self-transfer (`amount_mismatch`) and then rejected the fee payer also being
the payer (`fee_payer_transferring_hbar`). Both refusals were fixed by
introducing genuinely separate accounts, not by relaxing checks.

## Why it matters

Verification is not an API call — it is an economic transaction with a
consensus receipt, and the answer is cryptographically bound to one exact
action. Lift the signed result onto a different payment and the signature no
longer verifies.

Produced by `gate-x402-live.ts` using the official `@x402/core` facilitator
with `@x402/hedera`'s exact scheme; the facilitator is assembled from the
SDK's own building blocks (fee-payer signing with consensus wait, payer
signature verification against the on-chain account key, Mirror Node
preflight).
